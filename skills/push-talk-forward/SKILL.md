Advance the current discussion toward a complete, reviewable requirement. We have already established shared understanding. Build on that common ground:

1. **Advance design.** Resolve the remaining requirements design space using established context and your own judgment. Challenge your choices against the agreed constraints and meaningful alternatives. Continue until the design is coherent enough to specify, or further progress requires a major unresolved requirement or decision from the user.
2. **Show design.** Use `../show-me/SKILL.md` to visualize the proposed design.
3. **Explain decisions.** Explain the key decisions, assumptions, and trade-offs made while advancing the design. When useful, relate the problem to established engineering or research problems, their common solutions, and how this case differs. Distinguish what was previously agreed from what you decided or assumed.

Do not write a spec or begin implementation. The user decides whether to accept, revise, or reconsider the design before proceeding.
